BREAKFAST RECIPE

Chocolate Pancakes

Soft, fluffy and delicious chocolate pancakes that are easy to prepare and perfect for breakfast.

Preparation 10 min
Cooking 15 min
Servings 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix flour, cocoa powder, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla extract.
  3. Whisk the mixture until a smooth batter is formed.
  4. Heat a pan and lightly grease it with butter.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles appear.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter.
  8. Serve warm with chocolate sauce or fresh fruits.

Enjoy your homemade chocolate pancakes!
